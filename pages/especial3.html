Activity 2 Holiday Vocabulary

Instructions: Learn the names and dates of important holidays. Each card shows the emoji, name, and date of celebration.

Important Note:

  • Eve = Short form of "evening" - used to express the previous day of a big celebration
  • Example: Christmas Eve (December 24th) is the day before Christmas (December 25th)
  • Example: New Year's Eve (December 31st) is the day before New Year's Day (January 1st)
