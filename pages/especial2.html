🕐 What Time Is It?

Telling Time & Frequency Time Expressions

Learn to tell time and describe how often you do activities

📖 Theory

How to Tell Time:

Two Ways to Tell Time:

  • Digital format: "It's two fifteen." (2:15)
  • Traditional format: "It's a quarter past two." (2:15)
📋 Key Vocabulary for Telling Time:
  • o'clock = en punto (3:00 = three o'clock)
  • past = después de (3:15 = a quarter past three)
  • to = para / menos (3:45 = a quarter to four)
  • a quarter = 15 minutos
  • half = 30 minutos (3:30 = half past three)

Important Rules:

  • Use "in" for parts of the day: in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening
  • Use "at" for specific times: at noon, at night, at 5:00 a.m.
  • Use "on" for days: on Monday, on Saturday afternoon
  • Frequency expressions usually go at the end of the sentence

💡 Examples

Telling Time Examples:

It's two fifteen. / It's a quarter past two.
Son las dos y quince. / Es un cuarto después de las dos.
It's three thirty. / It's half past three.
Son las tres y treinta. / Son las tres y media.
It's four forty-five. / It's a quarter to five.
Son las cuatro y cuarenta y cinco. / Es un cuarto para las cinco.

Frequency Time Expressions Examples:

I take a shower every day.
Yo me baño todos los días.
I have lunch at noon.
Yo almuerzo al mediodía.
I go to bed at night.
Yo me voy a la cama en la noche.
I play soccer on Saturday afternoon.
Yo juego fútbol el sábado en la tarde.
I brush my teeth twice a day.
Yo me cepillo los dientes dos veces al día.
